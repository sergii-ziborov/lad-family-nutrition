# Ladlekin support

Ladlekin is a family meal planner for iPhone: one kitchen, a personal portion for each person.

**Contact:** sergii.ziborov@gmail.com. Publisher: Serhii Ziborov. We usually reply within two working days.

When you write, mention your iPhone model, the iOS version and the app version. Please do not send medical records.

## Common questions

- **Where is my data?** Menus, family profiles, the food diary and weight stay on your iPhone. The app has no account and no sign-in.
- **How are portions calculated?** From the day's menu energy and each person's profile. Children's portions follow their age and size. You can set any portion by hand.
- **Are nutrition figures exact?** No. They are estimates from USDA FoodData Central ingredient data and the listed amounts. Check packaging for allergens.
- **How do I delete everything?** Delete the app. iPhone removes all of its local data.
