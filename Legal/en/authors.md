# For Course Authors

Ladlekin is being developed as a publishing platform for nutrition-course creators. Instead of distributing static sheets, authors can offer structured recipes, menu-generation rules and flexible meal combinations, while users plan portions, shopping, actual intake and weight records in one place. This is an organisational tool, not a promise to deliver or supervise medical treatment.

## Paid courses and earnings

Eligible authors may propose courses for paid distribution and contractual compensation. Paid checkout and automatic payouts are not launched in the current App Store version. Sales begin only after an author agreement, content approval and compliant payment infrastructure are in place. Submitting a proposal does not start sales or create a right to payment.

Each agreement must specify course ownership, territories, price-setting authority, the royalty rate, calculation base, currency, payout schedule and threshold, payment fees, taxes, refunds, chargebacks, reporting, termination and treatment of existing purchasers. No royalty percentage, minimum income or payment date is implied by this page. An illustrative calculation is: agreed royalty rate × the agreement's eligible net receipts; the actual definitions come from the signed agreement. Apple storefront fees and taxes are not money automatically payable to an author.

## Publishing standards

Provide original or properly licensed text and images, ingredient amounts, yield, preparation steps, allergens, sources and the scope of any dietary rules. General diet advice must not claim guaranteed weight loss or cure disease. Clinical claims require appropriate expertise and evidence. Material for children, pregnancy and special medical diets needs additional review. Permissions to publish, translate, adapt and illustrate third-party materials must be documented.

Courses may be translated into English, Russian and Ukrainian and adapted into a generator rather than a compulsory calendar, as agreed with the author. Descriptions follow the dish. Quantities in the recipe are the amounts to cook and are not laboratory measurements. Content is checked before publication; users cannot currently upload public courses from the app. Reports of unsafe or infringing material go to the private publisher contact and are reviewed, with correction or removal where warranted.

## Privacy and participation

Authors do not receive users' personal weight, health profiles, allergies or diaries merely because a course was selected. Any future aggregate sales reporting must respect applicable privacy law. Proposals should include a course summary, rights information, sample recipes and desired commercial model, sent through the official support contact published before programme launch. Bank and tax information must not be submitted in a public issue tracker.
