# Sources and Support

The current recipe-card text was prepared for Ladlekin from ingredient and cooking-method facts. Dish illustrations show a serving suggestion rather than a precisely weighed cooked portion.

Cooking temperatures were checked against [FoodSafety.gov](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures) and [USDA FSIS](https://ask.fsis.usda.gov/article/To-what-temperature-should-I-cook-beef). Timing varies with food size and equipment; checking doneness takes priority over a timer.

Version: 26 October 2026. Ladlekin is a meal-planning and personal-record tool. ## Nutrition and food safety

[USDA FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/) supplies ingredient reference composition. [USDA nutrient retention factors](https://www.ars.usda.gov/ARSUserFiles/80400525/Data/retn/retn06.pdf) inform applicable preparation estimates. Nutrient-role explanations reference [NIH Office of Dietary Supplements](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/list-all/) and [FDA nutrition information](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-facts-label/how-understand-and-use-nutrition-facts-label). Storage guidance references [FoodSafety.gov](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/cold-food-storage-charts) and [FoodKeeper](https://www.foodsafety.gov/keep-food-safe/foodkeeper-app). Adult maintenance-energy estimates follow [DRI Energy 2023](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/26818/chapter/7); they do not automatically prescribe a weight-loss deficit.

The healthy-eating course draws general food-pattern ideas from [American Heart Association](https://www.heart.org/en/healthy-living/healthy-eating/eat-smart/nutrition-basics/mediterranean-diet). None of these organisations endorses Ladlekin. Ingredient labels and individual professional advice take precedence over general app information.

## Illustrations and software

Ingredient and recipe photos are illustrations of the dish, checked against the recipe. They are not a photograph of a user's food. Ladlekin uses native SwiftUI and Swift Charts. ## Reference prices

Selected statistical food-price categories come from [Israel CBS](https://www.cbs.gov.il/he/publications/Madad/DocLib/2026/price10a/a5_6_h.xlsx), the [State Statistics Service of Ukraine](https://stat.gov.ua/uk/datasets/zminy-tsin-taryfiv-na-spozhyvchi-tovary-posluhy), and [U.S. BLS](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/factsheets/average-prices.htm). CBS data follows its [reuse license](https://www.cbs.gov.il/he/cbsNewBrand/Pages/רישיון-שימוש.aspx); Ukrainian data uses [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); BLS data is [public domain](https://www.bls.gov/opub/copyright-information.htm). Each price shows its period and source category. Pack units are standardised for portion costs; currencies are not converted. Brands, varieties and fat content may differ. Your own prices take priority. Missing prices are not treated as zero. The providers are not partners and do not endorse the app.
