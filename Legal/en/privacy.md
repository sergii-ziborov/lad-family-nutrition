# Privacy Policy

Version: 21 October 2026. Scope: the first App Store release of Ladlekin. Publisher identity and a private support/privacy contact must be added to the store listing and the public policy before publication. This policy does not authorise selling personal data.

## Information you enter

Family names or nicknames, age, height, weight, allergies, dietary preferences, menus, food records, supplement notes, ingredient stocks and prices are processed to provide the features you choose. Fields not needed for your chosen feature may be left empty. A locally generated identifier distinguishes records on this device; it is not used for advertising.

## Local health records

The App Store build stores personal records in an iOS-protected local file and excludes that file and week archives from device backup. It does not upload health profiles, food diaries or weight history through CloudKit or the catalogue API. HealthKit is not connected. The pilot iCloud switch is unavailable in this release. Historical pilot cloud records or backups are not automatically deleted; manage existing copies using your Apple Account controls. Export important local records before removing the app or changing devices.

## Catalogue requests

Opening and refreshing the public catalogue downloads course text and illustrations over HTTPS. The hosting provider and service necessarily receive an IP address and technical connection information. Routine access logging is disabled in the current server configuration; security or error diagnostics may contain technical request details. Do not enter private health information in catalogue addresses. Final privacy-label answers must match the deployed logging configuration. Local weight, height, allergies and diary entries are not part of these catalogue requests.

## Notifications and sharing

Reminders require your permission and can be turned off in settings. Lock-screen notifications do not show food names, calories or health measurements. The free edition may display non-personalized Google AdMob ads in the catalogue. The SDK may process an IP address, technical device information and ad impression or interaction events. Ladlekin does not send weight, height, allergies, goals, diary entries or selected dishes to the advertising SDK, and does not request cross-app tracking. A consent form is shown when required; ad privacy options are available in settings. The purchase disables advertising. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Purchase verification sends a signed Apple transaction to the catalogue server, without personal diary records or payment details. Recipe photos in the catalogue are illustrations of the dish. CSV export happens only when you choose a recipient in the system share sheet. Once shared, that recipient controls its copy under its own policy.

## Retention and control

Local records are kept until you edit or delete them or remove the app. You can remove individual diary or weight records, change profile fields, turn off notifications and export your diary or weight CSV. Removing a weight measurement does not rewrite the current profile weight. Disabling a pilot cloud preference does not erase older remote copies. Retain only information you want stored on a shared device; use an iPhone passcode and handle exported files carefully.

## Families, requests and changes

Adults should manage children's profiles with appropriate permission and professional guidance. The app is not a child-directed social network and has no public profile, messaging or author access to your health records. For applicable access, correction, deletion or privacy requests, use the private contact published in the App Store support page. Do not post health records in public issue trackers. Material changes to data use require an updated policy and any required permission; this version's date identifies its scope.
